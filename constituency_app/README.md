## 🎯 The Problem

Indian Members of Parliament face an overwhelming challenge managing constituency demands:

- 📂 **5,000+ grievances/year** submitted across multiple formats and languages.
- 💰 **₹5 crore MPLADS budget** distributed without data-driven tools.
- 🏗️ **30–50 active projects** running simultaneously with zero real-time progress monitoring.
- 📉 **Zero constituency insights** available for proactive, informed governance.

> **Result:** 30-day average response time, 2,000+ backlog, and only 35% citizen satisfaction.

---

## 💡 Our Solution

An integrated **AI-Powered Command Center** designed for MPs, district collectors, and administrators to streamline grievance redressal, optimize developmental funds, and track civic infrastructure in real time.

---

## ✨ Key Features

### 🎤 1. Intelligent Grievance Management
* **Multimodal Submissions:** Citizens can submit complaints via voice, text, images, or site videos.
* **12 Indian Languages:** Automatic translation and natural language understanding.
* **AI Categorization & Deduplication:** Gemini AI tags issues and groups duplicate complaints automatically.
* **Smart Prioritization:** Ranks issues dynamically based on severity, population affected, and sentiment.

### 💰 2. AI Resource Allocation Optimizer
* **MPLADS Fund Planning:** Generates high-impact allocation recommendations for the ₹5 Cr annual budget.
* **Impact Scoring:** Calculates citizen beneficiaries per rupee invested.
* **Equity Analysis:** Prevents biased development by ensuring balanced spending across all wards and blocks.
* **Compliance Checks:** Automatic validation against official government procurement guidelines.

### 📊 3. Smart Project Monitoring
* **Real-Time RAG Status:** Instant visual health check (Red/Amber/Green) for all constituency works.
* **AI Image Verification:** Inspects on-site photo submissions to detect fake progress reports.
* **Milestone & Delay Prediction:** Flags contractor delays before deadlines are breached.

### 🤖 4. Constituency Intelligence Assistant
* **Natural Language Queries:** Ask questions like *"Which villages lack primary health centers?"* for instant data.
* **Automated Daily Briefings:** Executive AI summaries prepared every morning for the MP's office.
* **Predictive Trends:** Anticipates water shortages, road damage, or civic issues before they escalate.
